G14 = G12 + bump_image_registration true, bump_image_max_rotation_correction_deg 2.0,
      bump_image_rotation_fallback_window 10, bump_image_rotation_fallback_min_count 3 (rko_lio 400fc3c, ws/rko_g13;
      the other bump_image_* keys at their defaults, BIEVR-LIO's map parameters). Each scan is registered against
      voxel-wise height ("bump") images of the map, using a 0.1 m downsample of the scan in the 300 map voxels with the
      most relief; a scan whose bump result turns more than 2 deg away from the IMU guess in at least 3 of the last 10
      scans is registered again point-to-point (the ENWIDE v10 fallback, configs/enwide/rko_lio_os0_open_ground_v10.yaml).

it has transient vertical drifts (ARC-5) and start failures (PIL-2). In the screen (one run each, ATE without reference
spikes, m; G12 / G14 / BIEVR-LIO): ARC-5 0.0554 / 0.0120 / 0.0147, CON-3 0.0281 / 0.0164 / 0.0162,
PIL-2 0.0768 / 0.0294 / 0.0297, HEAP-1 0.0281 / 0.0281 / 0.0285, SPX-3 0.0097 / 0.0091 / 0.0080; vertical jitter
(p1_grandtour_jitter.py) 18.5 -> 6.9, 15.3 -> 7.0, 18.0 -> 9.5, 9.0 -> 7.4, 5.6 -> 5.1 mm.

Adopt G14 as G if all of these hold (one run each, frontend only), checked in this order, stopping at the first that
fails:
  1. GrandTour HS rig, the 15 missions above: mean below G12's 0.0422, and no mission worse than G12 by more than
     10 % and more than 2 mm at once (on missions near 1 cm, 10 % is 1 mm). snow-1 is run and reported only.
  2. The Phase 1 gate: each family's geometric mean within 1.10 x its best profile (MapsHD's eight, Oxford 0.0503,
     exp04 0.0520), the all-ten geometric mean within 1.05 x 0.0348 and not above G12's 0.0353.
  3. Koide: no sequence worse than G12 by more than 5 %, and 01a below 1.5 m.
  4. GrandTour LS rig, the 8 missions above: mean not above G12's 0.0401, and no mission worse than G12 by more than
     10 % and more than 2 mm at once.
  5. Every run real-time (node time below the data duration).
If criterion 1 holds and a later one fails, G14 is not adopted as G; whether bump registration may then be switched by
a rig property (a spinning multi-beam LiDAR) is a new candidate with its own rule.
If G14 is adopted, the HS rig with G14 becomes the COMFORT candidate; a submission needs a new preregistration and the
maintainer's decision.
