Phase 1, G21: the three tilt keys of G19 as a per-rig input for tactical-grade gyros (the maintainer's decision of
2026-11-09, made with the G19 runs and the G20 screen below known; development data only).

G21 = G12 (configs/sota_v7/rko_lio_global_G12_2026-10-26.yaml, unchanged) plus, for a rig whose gyro is tactical grade,
the per-rig file configs/sota_v7/rig_tactical_gyro_2026-11-09.yaml (md5 6f193ab670dea8d1ecf53a773761e274):
max_tilt_correction_deg 0.05, gyro_bias_time_constant_sec 10.0, gyro_bias_start_sec 30.0 (G19's keys). Like the
gravity window (report chapter 30.9), it is a rig input chosen from the IMU's own specification, never from a
reference trajectory, and it is off for every other rig.

Tactical grade: the gyro's datasheet publishes an angle random walk of at most 0.2 deg/sqrt(h) and an in-run bias
instability of at most 1 deg/h. The rigs of the development and holdout data:
  STIM320 (GrandTour HS, LS and merged rigs; H5): 0.10-0.15 deg/sqrt(h), 0.3-0.4 deg/h (Safran product page and
    Sensonor product brief, which differ slightly)                                           -> on
  Bosch BMI085 (Alphasense Core: HILTI 2022 exp04 and H4, Oxford Spires): noise density 0.014 deg/s/sqrt(Hz)
    = 0.84 deg/sqrt(h), bias instability below 2 deg/h (Bosch flyer)                         -> off
  TDK ICM-40609-D (Livox MID-360 built-in: MapsHD): 4.5 mdps/sqrt(Hz) = 0.27 deg/sqrt(h), no bias instability
    published (TDK product page)                                                             -> off
  Livox built-in IMU of Koide outdoor_hard (consumer MEMS)                                   -> off
  OXTS RT3003 (KITTI raw): no published gyro figures found                                   -> off until one is
Off is the default: a rig without published figures runs G12.

Why (known before the decision):
  G19 (= G21 on a STIM320 rig) on GrandTour, decision_rule_G19.txt Record: HS 15 mean 0.0421 -> 0.0289 m, LS 8 mean
  0.0401 -> 0.0376 m, no mission outside the tolerance, real-time; G19 failed only the Phase 1 gate, on MapsHD's
  ICM-40609 (family ratio 1.108 > 1.10). G21 is G12 on every Phase 1 rig (MapsHD, exp04, Oxford, Koide), so the
  gate is G12's.
  G20 screen (not preregistered, not adopted; screen_g20/): the tilt limit at least c x the median of the run's own
  roll/pitch corrections (max_tilt_correction_median_factor, rko_lio 3761efd). On MapsHD's eight the family ratio
  falls to 1.040 / 1.027 / 1.014 (c = 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0); on GrandTour HS c = 1.0 stays with G19 and c >= 1.5 puts arc-4
  outside the tolerance against G12 (0.0258, 0.0267 against 0.0233); on the LS rig c = 1.0 loses arc-3's stairs
  (0.0280 -> 0.0499), so LS 8's mean would sit at G12's. The median correction cannot tell a poor gyro (MapsHD,
  0.16 deg per moving scan) from a noisy registration with a good gyro (MID-360 points on the STIM320: 0.13 deg);
  the gyro's own grade can.
