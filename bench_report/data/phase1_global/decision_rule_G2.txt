Phase 1, candidate G2 (fixed 2026-10-26 before any split-kernel run).

G2 = V + icp_robust_kernel_scale_factor 2.0 (chosen for G1 on the MapsHD 4-trial screen; not re-tuned)
     + icp_robust_kernel_split_horizontal true: correspondences whose matched voxel normal is within about 25 deg
       of the world vertical (|n_z| >= 0.9) take the kernel scale of their own median, the others theirs; a class
       with fewer than 20 correspondences in an iteration falls back to the scale of all of them.
Hypothesis (chapter 30): rough ground has large residuals and loses the height constraint under one scale.

(8 MapsHD trials, Oxford Keble 05, exp04 seven points) <= 1.10 x its reference (docs/comparison.md frontend
