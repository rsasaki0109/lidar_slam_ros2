G18 = G12 + max_tilt_correction_deg 0.05 + gyro_bias_time_constant_sec 10.0 (the other keys of G12 unchanged;
gyro_bias_max_correction_deg stays at its default 1.0). File: data/phase1_global/rko_lio_candidate_G18_2026-11-09.yaml
(md5 e9f42298c4924b68f0028bc714f1b6a2). Build: rko_lio f2346e3, installed in this repository and in /home/user/ws/rko_g18
(offline_node md5 e41f112470558743f5785ccd9a690c08 in both). After each registration the change it makes to the
gyro-predicted roll and pitch is limited to 0.05 deg (G17); then the gyro bias moves by -delta / 10 s, delta being the
correction the scan keeps of the gyro-predicted attitude in the body frame (a first-order low-pass of the
registration's rate correction).

Why (report chapter 60.4, rko_lio's per-scan attitude diagnostic RKO_LIO_TILT_DIAG, data/phase1_global/screen_g18/):
  G17 lost where the initialization's gyro bias was wrong. The bias is the mean gyro rate of the 0.1-s initialization
  interval (50 STIM320 samples); a standing legged robot sways, and that average was 6.4 mrad/s on HS con-2 and
  13.5 mrad/s on LS spx-1. In G12 the registration corrects exactly that every scan (body-frame mean correction
  -5.8 / -2.2 / -2.7 mrad/s on con-2 against an initial bias -5.3 / -2.3 / -2.9; -13.5 / 0.1 / -2.1 on LS spx-1
  against -13.3 / 0.2 / -2.1), so the STIM320's own bias is near zero; with G17's limit the correction is cut and the
  world frame tilts. PIL-1's stairs are different: there the initial bias is small (0.7 mrad/s) and the registration
  itself pulls the tilt (mean 0.024 deg/scan at 375-390 s; per-scan tilt correction 0.22 deg rms at 360-390 s against
  0.04-0.10 elsewhere); the tilt information of the ICP Hessian (Schur complement) does not drop there.
  Tracking the bias without the limit lets the registration's drift into the bias (HS arc-3 0.4275 m).
