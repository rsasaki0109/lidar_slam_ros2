KITTI 05: correct the Velodyne's elevation offset, estimated from the scans alone (a candidate for KITTI's data
preparation; G does not change). Written 2026-11-01, before the estimate and before any run.

Why (chapter 48, error_breakdown_rotdeskew.txt): after the rotation deskew the frontend still drifts down by 0.47 to
0.81 % of the distance (signed mean vertical error -0.717 % over 200 m windows, about equal to its RMS). A Velodyne
whose beams all point lower than their calibration says makes flat ground look like a cone falling away from the
sensor (z measured = z true - r tan(delta)), and scan-to-map registration then drifts vertically. Such an offset on
KITTI's HDL-64 is known (IMLS-SLAM 0.22 deg, KISS-ICP's KITTI loader 0.205 deg); those values are context only and are
not used.

Estimate (bench_report/scripts/p2_kitti_elevation_offset.py, on the deskewed bag, no ground truth): every 5th scan;
points of the ground along the car, |y| < 3 m and 5 m < |x| < 40 m with z within [-2.2, -1.2] m of the sensor, fitted
by z = a + c x + b |x| with three rounds of keeping the points within 0.15 m of the fit. |x| is even and x odd in
the driving direction, so b is the cone and c the tilt of the car or road; the road's camber across it is left out by
the narrow band. A scan counts if at least 300 points remain on each side of the car. delta = -atan(median of b over
the scans). Whatever delta comes out is used; it is not adjusted afterwards.

Candidate: kitti_raw_to_ros2.py with ELEVATION_CORRECTION_DEG=delta as well as ROTATION_DESKEW=1 rotates each point
by delta about the axis (p x z) / |p x z| (raising its elevation by delta, as KISS-ICP's correct_kitti_scan does),
before the rotation deskew. The frontend diagnostic (p2_kitti_front_diag.sh) runs on that bag.

Reading, fixed now
R1. The signed mean vertical error over 200 m windows (p2_kitti_error_breakdown.py) falls by at least half (from
    -0.717 % to -0.36 % or closer to 0).
R2. APE RMSE and t_rel get no more than 2 % worse than the rotation-deskewed run (2.109 m, 0.8444 %).
If both hold, KITTI's data preparation applies the estimated delta (p2_kitti_raw.sh); otherwise nothing changes.

Estimate (2026-11-01, p2_kitti_elevation_offset.py on the rotation-deskewed bag, every 5th scan): 553 of 2,762 scans
used; median slope b = -0.002106, delta = +0.1206 deg (interquartile +0.0119 .. +0.1872 deg; the mean of b gives
+0.0237 deg, pulled by outlying scans). The sign is the literature's (beams lower than calibrated); the size is about
60 % of 0.205 deg. delta = 0.1206 deg is used, as fixed above.
