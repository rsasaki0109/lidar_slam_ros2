Map update for joined sessions, step 1: drop an earlier session's points that a later session saw through
(fixed 2026-11-08 before any run of bench_report/scripts/p6_map_change.py).

Why
  A joined map keeps every session's points. Where the place changed between visits (a moved car, a new wall gap, a
  person standing), the earlier session's points stay as ghosts in space the later session saw as empty.

Method (p6_map_change.py, the constants at its top; not tuned on these data)
  The old map: every 5th frame of the old session's backend input, in the reference frame, one point per 0.1 m voxel.
  The new session's rays: every 5th frame, one ray per 0.2 m voxel of the scan (1-40 m), from the sensor to the point,
  sampled every 0.1 m up to 0.3 m before the point. An old point is stale when its 0.2 m voxel was crossed by rays of at
  least 2 new frames and no new point lies in that voxel or one of its 26 neighbours (the neighbours keep surfaces seen
  at grazing angles).

Data: RTK-SLAM Construction 1 and 2 (/home/user/bench/ms_cons, report chapter 56.2), recorded about 13 minutes apart on
  one day with the same MID-360 rig. Poses: each session's corrected per-frame trajectory (replay/*/dense.tum), session 2
  mapped into session 1's frame by the join's transform (runs/j12, joined).

Evaluations
  E1 (real points, little change): old = session 1, new = session 2, and the reverse (old = session 2 in session 1's
     frame, new = session 1). Measure: the fraction of the old map's points removed. Between two visits 13 minutes apart
     only moving people and machines should go.
  E2 (known stale points): the same two runs with --ghosts: 0.4 m cubes of points (0.05 m spacing on the faces) added
     to the old map on the new session's trajectory every 10 m of its path, where the new sensor itself passed, so
     the new session saw that space empty. Measure: the fraction of the cubes' points removed (recall).
  Reported besides: the fraction of the old map in voxels the new rays crossed at least twice, the removed fraction of
  those, the time, and top-down images of the removed points.
Decision: the method goes on to a product option (an opt-in step of `lidarslam-map merge --place-recognition`, which
  has every run's backend input) if, in both directions, E1 removes at most 1.0 % of the old map and E2 removes at least
  90 % of the cube points. Otherwise it is recorded as not good enough and the product does not change.

Record
  2026-11-08, step 1 (p6_map_change.py at 303d1bbc), Construction 1 + 2, 1,175 / 1,457 new frames used, 77 / 85 s:
    old 1, new 2: removed 1.08 % of 2,009,376 old points (1.74 % of those in voxels the new rays crossed twice; 62 % of
      the old map is in such voxels); cubes: 58.3 % of 16,598 points in 43 cubes removed.
    old 2, new 1: removed 1.27 % of 1,732,453 (1.87 % of the crossed; 68 % crossed); cubes: 66.2 % of 20,458 in 53.
  Both criteria fail in both directions. Decision: not good enough; the product does not change.
  Why (looked at after the decision; images old1_new2.png, old2_new1.png): the removed points form dotted trails along
  the old session's path. 78 % of them lie within 2 m of the old path, within about 1 m of the sensor in its own frame
  (body-frame offsets p10-p90: x -0.76 to 0.46, y -0.96 to 0.83, z -0.69 to 0.40 m): the carrier of the sensor, seen just
  beyond the 1 m minimum range in every frame. The new session saw through those places, so removing them is right, but
  they are not the change the method is for, and they count against E1. The same points of the new session lie next to
  its own path, so they protect the planted cubes there (one-voxel neighbourhood), which is why the cubes' recall is low.

Step 2 plan (fixed 2026-11-08 before any run of it)

  the base frame of the backend input; identity, as before, when omitted), for a rig whose scans are in the LiDAR frame.
  The 1.5 m comes from step 1's look at Construction, so Construction is no longer independent of it. A second pair is

  Evaluations E1 and E2 as in step 1, both directions, on both pairs (four runs).
  Decision: as step 1 (E1 at most 1.0 %, E2 at least 90 %), required in all four runs; reported for each pair apart.
