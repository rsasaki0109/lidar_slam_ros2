Whole-map check scaled by each session's own map consistency. Written 2026-11-07, after chapters 52 and 53 and before
any run with the new measure. A measurement and a candidate rule, not an adoption: nothing changes a default.

Question
- Chapter 52: on two outdoor MID-360 walks of one park (RTK-SLAM Stadtgarten 1 + 2) the whole-map check refused right
  sets (10.2 % and 18.8 % of places below half overlap, limit 10 %; chapter 53: 0.28 m and 0.38 m from the truth). The
  Construction pair's wrong sets, which the check was made to refuse, score in the same range (13-30 % below, median
  0.62-0.65; chapter 38 step 4). No threshold on these two numbers separates them.
- Hypothesis: a place's overlap is limited by how well the site's maps repeat themselves at all. Foliage and sparse
  returns keep an outdoor walk's maps from overlapping even with the same walk's own maps a few metres away, while a
  building's maps overlap almost fully. A bar relative to that ceiling can accept outdoor right sets and still refuse
  a building's wrong sets.

Measure (graph_slam_multisession_join, off by default, reported in join_report.yaml)
- Consistency of a session S, c_S: for every keyframe k of S, the share of k's points within inlier_distance_m (0.3 m)
  of the maps of S's other keyframes whose positions lie within place_radius_m (3 m) of k's; keyframes with no such
  neighbour are skipped; c_S is the median over the keyframes (the same points, distances and nearest-neighbour search
  as the whole-map check).
- Scaled bar: a place counts as below when its fraction is below place_min_fraction x min(1, c_pair / c0), with
  c_pair = min(c_A, c_B). All other values unchanged (10 % of places, 3 m, 0.3 m, at least 5 places, the ambiguity and
  self-similarity refusals).
- c0 is fixed from the Construction pair, where the current bar of 0.5 was set: c0 = min(c_con-1, c_con-2), measured
  before any join with the scaled bar is run. A pair at least as consistent as Construction keeps the bar of 0.5.
