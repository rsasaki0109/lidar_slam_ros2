What is built
- multisession_gnss_gate (default false). GNSS is read per session from multisession_gnss_topic (/gnss/fix) in the
  session's bag (backend_input), or from multisession_gnss_bag_paths; the antenna offset is
  multisession_gnss_antenna_xyz. Fixes, pairing with the odometry, the local ENU frame, the 30 m windows, the trimmed
  4-DoF fits and the per-place position and heading disagreement are as plan_local.txt / plan_rotation.txt; the
  thresholds are the join's ambiguity_m and ambiguity_deg.
- When a set tried has at least 5 measured places, GNSS decides the join: the first set (largest first) whose median
  position and heading disagreement are within the thresholds joins, without the whole-map verdict, the ambiguity and
  the self-similarity refusals. When no set has 5 measured places, the join is decided as today.
