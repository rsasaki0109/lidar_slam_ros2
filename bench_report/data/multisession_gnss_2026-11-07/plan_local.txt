A local GNSS gate. Written 2026-11-07, after chapter 56 and before any local GNSS comparison below was computed. Nothing
changes a default.

Question
- Chapter 56's gate failed because one rigid fit of GNSS to a session's odometry inherits the session's drift: the two
  halves of Stadtgarten 1 differed by 0.62 deg in yaw, 1.2 m at B's keyframes. Does a comparison made place by place,
  with GNSS fitted only around each place, avoid that and keep the rest of chapter 56's results?

The local gate (fixed now; fixes, pairing, antenna and the trimmed 4-DoF fit as in plan.txt)
- Places of a transform T (a set's transform or a checked one): B's keyframes that T puts within 3 m of one of A's
  keyframes (the whole-map check's places), each with the nearest A keyframe.
- At each place: the A pairs whose odometry antenna position is within 30 m of that A keyframe and the B pairs within
  30 m of the B keyframe; with at least 50 of each, the two trimmed fits give the local T_loc = T_enu<-A^-1 T_enu<-B,
  and the place's disagreement is |T p_b - T_loc p_b| at the B keyframe's position p_b. Fewer pairs: not measured.
- Gate: at least 5 measured places, the median disagreement at most 1.0 m, and the whole-map floor of plan.txt (at
  least 5 places, at most 25 % below 0.5). The first set accepted (largest first) joins; with GNSS the ambiguity and
  self-similarity refusals are not applied.

Data: as chapter 56 (Stadtgarten 1 <- 2 and 2 <- 1 with their sets and chapter 55's 24 transforms each; Stadtgarten 1 and
2 split, as is and with the matches within 2 m of the identity removed). Truth: the checkpoint placement; the identity
for a split.

Reading, fixed now
1. Usable: at the truth, the median disagreement is at most 0.5 m in both pair orders and on the Stadtgarten 1 split.
2. Accept: the gate accepts the largest set in both pair orders and the Stadtgarten 1 split's set (all right sets).
3. Refuse: the gate accepts no chapter-55 transform moved by 2 m or more or turned by 5 deg or more. Reported besides:
   the 1 m and 2 deg transforms, how many of them the median alone refuses, and the measured places per case.
4. If 1-3 hold, the local gate is a candidate to build into the join (reading GNSS from backend_input) and to check end to
   end on the product path; wrong sets with GNSS (none on disk) stay untested and are named. If any fails, it is not
   built and the reason is named.

Result (2026-11-07; local_*.json; bench_report/scripts/p3_ms_gnss_local.py)
- At the truth: median disagreement 0.277 m (1 <- 2, 246 of 256 places measured), 0.378 m (2 <- 1, 303 of 313), 0.298 m
  (Stadtgarten 1 split, 27 of 27). The Stadtgarten 2 split has no place (its halves do not meet).
- Sets: 1 <- 2 largest set median 0.285 m, accepted (0.284 m from the truth); 2 <- 1 largest set 0.485 m, accepted
  (0.378 m). Stadtgarten 1 split: its one set (0.049 m from the identity) has median 0.289 m, but the whole-map floor
  refuses it (37 % of its 27 places below 0.5, the floor allows 25 %), so the gate refuses it. The pairs' second and
  third sets fail the floor (33-51 %); the third set of 2 <- 1 also fails the median (1.115 m).
- Chapter 55's transforms: the gate accepts none but the joined one. The median alone refuses every transform moved by
  2 m or more or turned by 2 deg or more (medians 1.8-133 m), and 6 of the 8 moved by 1 m (+x 1 m passes it in both
  orders: 0.97 / 0.94 m, refused by the floor).
- Splits with the matches near the identity removed: no set (as in chapter 56).
Reading
1. Met (0.28, 0.38, 0.30 m).
2. NOT met: the Stadtgarten 1 split's right set is refused, by the whole-map floor, not by GNSS.
3. Met: nothing 2 m or 5 deg off is accepted (nor 2 deg).
4. The local gate is not built as it stands. The reason is the floor carried over from plan.txt: the local GNSS
   comparison itself met every reading. A gate on the local comparison alone (no floor) would, on these cases, accept
   all three right sets and refuse every transform 2 m or 2 deg off; that is read after the fact and needs data not
   used here (another pair of sessions with RTK GNSS) before it can be a candidate. Wrong sets with GNSS: none on disk.
