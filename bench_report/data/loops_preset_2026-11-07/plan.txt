Walking preset loop registration: NDT 2 m (current) or small_gicp 0.5 m. Written 2026-11-07, before any run of this
plan. Nothing below was measured when it was written.

Question
- Should lidarslam/param/lidarslam_mid360_rko_graph_walking.yaml switch its loop registration from
  registration_method NDT (ndt_resolution 2.0) to SMALL_GICP with gicp_max_correspondence_distance 0.5 and
  gicp_transformation_epsilon 1e-4 ("sg05", as in chapters 36, 37 and 49)? Every other key of the preset stays,
  including ndt_num_threads 1 (SMALL_GICP's thread count too).
- Why now: graph_based_slam vendors small_gicp since PR #593, so a preset can rely on it. Chapter 37 did not recommend
  sg05 (its rule "10-sequence geometric mean below the frontend alone" missed by 0.0001 m) and noted that a preset could
  not rely on small_gicp then.

Evidence already seen (development data; listed for the reader, not re-scored, and not enough alone because it was seen)
- Chapter 36, GrandTour 6 missions: sg05 at most +0.8 mm against the frontend (NDT 2 m +6.6 mm), no loop over 0.1 m off.
- Chapter 37, 10 sequences: sg05 at most +2.7 % against the frontend, geometric mean 0.0354 m (NDT 2 m 0.0364, frontend
  0.0353); NDT 2 m +22 % and +16 % on underground-garage 2 and 3. Oxford Keble 05 was development data then; its ground
  truth is sealed now and is not opened by this plan.
- Chapter 37 cost: on Oxford, sg05 took 1,102 s backend-only against NDT 2 m's 282 s (581 s of data, 1 thread).
- Chapter 49, KITTI 05: APE RMSE sg05 0.802 m, NDT 2 m 1.096 m, frontend 1.960 m; KITTI 07: 0.4705 / 0.4696 / 0.4710 m.

- Time: the bag pass, from the runner's first log line to "Offline run complete". Runs one at a time on this VM, with
  nothing else running.

Rule, fixed now
R1 Accuracy. On each Stadtgarten sequence where at least one arm adds a loop edge:
   (a) sg05 <= 1.05 x ndt2 (not worse than the current preset by more than 5 %), and
   (b) sg05 <= 1.05 x the frontend alone (does not break the map; chapter 37's tolerance).
   A sequence where neither arm adds a loop edge is reported and does not count. If neither sequence counts, R1 is
   not met: there is no fresh evidence.
R2 Determinism. Two sg05 runs on the same input give byte-identical trajectory_optimized.tum and loop_edges.csv, on
   Stadtgarten seq2 and on KITTI 07.
R3 Cost. On each of the four inputs, sg05's bag pass <= 1.5 x ndt2's. On the two Stadtgarten (walking) inputs, also
   sg05's bag pass <= max(data duration, ndt2's bag pass): the live loop search keeps up wherever the current preset
   does.
Decision: switch the walking preset to sg05 only if R1, R2 and R3 all hold. Otherwise keep NDT 2 m and report which
part failed. If adopted: the preset, its header comment (its 0.0358 m figure is for the NDT backend), the tests that pin
the preset, and the docs. No other preset changes here; the GNSS, KITTI and default presets are separate decisions.
