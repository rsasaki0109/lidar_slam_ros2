Phase 3 (SOTA v7), robust loops on the other development data (2026-10-29): MapsHD (8 trials), Oxford Spires Keble 05,
HILTI 2022 exp04. Plan and reading fixed before any run. Follows bench_report/data/grandtour/loops_robust_2026-10-29/
(chapter 36): on GrandTour, small_gicp's GICP at a 0.5 m correspondence distance was the first loop registration that
made no mission more than 1 mm worse, but it did not help there, because a 2-cm frontend is as accurate as the loops.
These ten sequences drift more and are where the walking backend preset (NDT 2 m) was adopted (chapters 14-25).

(scripts/densify_corrected_trajectory.py, as in chapters 13-25), then scored as the frontend is in Phase 1 (MapsHD:
front_score.py, evo_ape -a in the LiDAR frame; Oxford: ape_oxford.py; exp04: ape_from_tum.py --interpolate on the
seven control points). The frontend of the same run is scored the same way.
Reading, per variant:
  1. it does not hurt: no sequence's APE more than 5 % above the frontend's;
  2. it helps: the geometric mean of the ten APEs is below the frontend's.
  A variant that meets both and whose geometric mean is not above ndt2's is recommended as the walking preset's loop
  registration in place of NDT 2 m. The preset file is not changed in this step (SMALL_GICP needs small_gicp in the
  build, which the preset cannot assume); that is put to the maintainer.
Reported besides: loop edges and backend wall time per sequence. The public MID-360 driving bag (whose start loop the
preset must keep closing) is not on this machine and is not run.
Disk: one sequence at a time (frontend, recording, three backend runs, scores), then its backend input is deleted.

Diagnosis (p3_mapshd_loop_errors.py, loop edges against MapsHD's 6-DoF ground truth): NDT's loops in the garages are
90-110 mm off in the median (garage 2: 98 mm, up to 251 mm) where the frontend's relative pose for the same pairs is
14-28 mm; small_gicp's are 14-34 mm there, about the frontend's. On calibration-room 1 small_gicp's 18 loops are better
than the frontend in translation (median 21.9 against 24.8 mm, 11 of 18) but worse in rotation (0.46 against 0.36 deg),
which is where its +2.7 % comes from. DCS on Oxford: the loops are within 7.3 cm of the frontend (chi2 at most about 1
in translation with the constant information 200), yet DCS with Phi 1 weakens their corrections (median 0.026 -> 0.015
m) and the result is worse than the frontend alone: Phi is not scaled to the edges' information.
Cost: small_gicp with the preset's one thread is slower on Oxford (backend 1102 s against NDT's 282 s for a 581-s
sequence; a live backend would fall behind); on MapsHD and exp04 it is as fast as NDT or faster (5-35 s).
