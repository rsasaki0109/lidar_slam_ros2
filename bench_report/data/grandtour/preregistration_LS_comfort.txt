Policy
  1. The six test trajectories are produced in one batch (p1_grandtour_batch.sh with IMU=stim320_imu) and packed into
     one zip; the md5 of every file is recorded here before the zip leaves the VM.
  2. The maintainer uploads it once to the test phase as submission 2. The scores are recorded whatever they are.
  3. As before: a pipeline failure is fixed and recorded; a poor trajectory because of G12 or the rig is submitted as is.
