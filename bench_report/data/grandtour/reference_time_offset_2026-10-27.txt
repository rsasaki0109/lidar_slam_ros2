Reading: every mission prefers a negative dt for all three methods, three independent estimators with their own
time handling, and the per-mission values move together (heap-1 about -20 ms for all three, con-2 and ice-1 about
-4 ms). The lag is in the reference's time chain (the total station's tracking), not in the estimators' stamps; it
varies with the mission. Shifting a submission's timestamps by a constant would fit the benchmark's reference latency
from the development ground truth, help every method alike and say nothing about the estimator: it is NOT applied to
any submission. Worth reporting to the organizers (the maintainer's decision); the frame-chain check on HEAP-1 (section 31.2,
50 ms steps, older G) was too coarse to see it.
