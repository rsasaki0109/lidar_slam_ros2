Phase 3, multi-session (SOTA v7 roadmap, docs/roadmap/sota-v7-plan-2026-10.md): join several runs of one site by
place recognition (2026-10-29). Development missions only; nothing here is for COMFORT.

Why GrandTour: it has several runs per site, and the total-station references of some share one frame, so a join can
be scored against the truth. Evidence (reference prism positions, no LiDAR): con-1 and con-2 (recorded 7 minutes apart)
walk the same corridors in the same reference coordinates (74 % of con-1's points within 0.5 m of con-2's path, median
0.23 m); arc-5 and arc-6 (14 minutes apart) the same (75 %, 0.15 m). Other pairs do not overlap in reference
coordinates (different areas or a different station set-up); they are not used as positive pairs.

Step 1 (this plan): a prototype (bench_report/scripts/p3_multisession_join.py, Python with small_gicp's wheel 1.0.1) that
finds the transform between two sessions' odometry frames with no prior, and refuses to join when it should not.
Inputs: each session's backend input (HS-G15g frontend's odometry and deskewed Hesai scans, thinned to 0.1 m; con-2's
from rl_g15g, con-1 / arc-5 / arc-6 recorded with the same frontend, label ms_g15g).
Truth: each session's frontend trajectory (at the prism) fitted to its reference (SE(3), spikes left out);
T_A<-B = T_ref<-A^-1 T_ref<-B. Before any join is scored, the truth is checked with the LiDAR data: B's keyframe maps put
into A's frame with it must lie on A's maps (median distance to A's nearest map, at places A has seen within 3 m).
Method, all values fixed here before any run:
  keyframes every 2.0 m of travel; a keyframe's map = the scans within +-1.0 s, in a frame at the keyframe's position
    with its yaw only (gravity-aligned; the odometry frame is), voxel 0.2 m, within 40 m;
  Scan Context (20 rings, 60 sectors, 30 m, cell = highest point + 2 m above the keyframe, at least 0); for each B
    keyframe the 3 A keyframes with the smallest Scan Context distance (best column shift), kept if the distance is at
    most 0.4;
  registration: small_gicp GICP, voxel 0.25 m, from the Scan Context yaw (translation 0), maximum correspondence
    distance 2.0, then 1.0, then 0.5 m;
  a match is accepted if at least 60 % of B's points have one of A's within 0.3 m after the registration;
  two matches agree if their session transforms move both matches' B keyframes to within 0.3 m of each other and differ
    by at most 1.0 deg; the largest set of mutually agreeing matches is found exactly (maximum clique);
  the join is accepted if that set has at least 3 matches whose B keyframes are at least 5 m apart from one another;
    its transform is the set's medoid (the member closest to the others).
Join error: the RMS, over B's trajectory, of the distance between B's positions moved by the estimated and by the true
transform.
Pairs: con-1 <- con-2 and arc-5 <- arc-6 (same frame; a join is expected); con-2 <- arc-6 and arc-5 <- con-1 (different
sites; a join would be wrong).
Reading: the prototype works if both same-site pairs are joined with an error of at most 0.10 m and neither
different-site pair is joined. Reported besides: candidates, accepted matches, how many of them are right (within 0.3 m
of the truth at their B keyframe), the largest agreeing set, run time. If it works, step 2 builds the same join into
graph_based_slam (inter-session edges in one pose graph) with its own plan.

Reading: met (both same-site pairs joined within 0.10 m, neither different-site pair joined).
But the margin is thin. On con-1 <- con-2, 34 accepted matches are wrong (2-33 m off), and 11 of them agree with one
another: a transform 8.31 m off, supported at 9 places of con-2 along 14 m of one corridor (the site's repeated
structure; the wrong matches cluster at 8.3 and 5.9 m). It contains 3 places 5 m apart, so on a pair whose true
overlap were missing it would be accepted as the join. The different-site pairs were easy negatives (no match
accepted at all).
Diagnosis of a whole-map check (on con-1 <- con-2, after the result): with a transform, every B keyframe that lands
within 3 m of an A keyframe is scored by the share of its points with one of A's (those A keyframes' maps) within 0.3 m.
The truth and the join: 109 places, median 0.99, 0 % of places below 0.5. The wrong set's transform: 72 places,
median 0.61, 31 % below 0.5.

Step 1b plan (fixed 2026-10-29 before any run with it). The join is accepted only if its transform also passes the
whole-map check: at least 5 B keyframes land within 3 m of an A keyframe, and at most 10 % of them have less than 50 %
of their points within 0.3 m of A's. If the largest agreeing set fails it, the largest set among the remaining
accepted matches is tried, up to 3 sets in all; if none passes, there is no join. Everything else is as in step 1.
The thresholds (5 places, 10 %, 50 %) were chosen from the diagnosis above on con-1 <- con-2, so that pair is
development data for them. Checked on:
  same-site, the reverse directions: con-2 <- con-1, arc-6 <- arc-5 (B sides not used for the thresholds);
  same-site, arc-4 with arc-5 both ways, if the truth check with the LiDAR maps puts arc-4's maps within 0.15 m of
    arc-5's (median); otherwise they are reported only;
  adversarial: each same-site pair with its right matches removed before the agreement step (the truth is used only to
    delete them, never in the decision), as if the sessions overlapped only where the site repeats itself;
  different-site: con-2 <- arc-6, arc-5 <- con-1 again.
Reading: every same-site pair joined within 0.10 m, and no adversarial or different-site pair joined.
