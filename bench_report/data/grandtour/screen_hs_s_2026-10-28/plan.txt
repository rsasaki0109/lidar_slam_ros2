Question: which part of the Hesai registration loses the height on these stairs. One change each on HS-G15g (rko_lio
d40d3b9, ws/rko_g35; the innovation limits stay off):
  hs/nb    bump_image_registration false (the bump image's height terms out);
  hs/v15   voxel_size 0.15 (finer map and keypoints than the 0.17-m risers; 0.25 now);
  hs/cs0   close_scene_max_median_range 0 (no close-scene settings: correspondence distance, localizability boost);
  hls/g15g the same file on the Hesai + Livox merged cloud;
  ls/g15g  the same file on the Livox cloud.
Runs: the test's arc-2 (GT-free; labels hss_*), and the development arc-3 (its three hidden stair passages and its
ATE). Reported per run: on arc-2 the heights above, the largest vertical speed over 0.3 s, the end level; on arc-3 the
height errors across the three gaps (climb_err) and the ATE without reference spikes; node time.
Reading, fixed now: a variant "keeps the arc-2 stairs" if it climbs 2.40-2.65 m by 125-140 s and has no vertical
speed above 1 m/s over 0.3 s. A variant that keeps them and whose arc-3 gap errors stay within 50 mm becomes a
candidate for a rule on all the development data (the 15 HS missions, tolerance and mean as decision_rule_G15g.txt).
Nothing here is adopted from arc-2 alone.
