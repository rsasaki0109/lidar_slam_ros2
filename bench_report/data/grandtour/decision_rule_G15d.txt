changes: over four stopping thresholds the means are 0.0170 (G15c) and 0.0171 (r5is).

G15d is NOT adopted; G15c stays the GrandTour HS configuration. Why spx-3 fails: its output leaves G15c's by 2-9 cm
in episodes of 5-25 s at the turns and comes back to 2-3 mm between them, offset mostly forward in the body frame:
the standing intervals of a static start set the accelerometer bias from the specific force against gravity in the
registered attitude, so an attitude error becomes a horizontal bias in the body frame that turns with the robot, and
the output filter's world-frame bias cannot follow it (con-3's output stays 8 cm from G15c's for 500 s instead, so
there the registration itself changed). The fourteenth screen (screen_hs_n_2026-10-28/) sets the gyro bias only.
