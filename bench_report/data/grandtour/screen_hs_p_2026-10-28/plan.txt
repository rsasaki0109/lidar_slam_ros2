Sixteenth screen on the HS rig: the output filter's measurement noise from each scan's registration (2026-10-28,
written before any adaptive replay; replayed offline like filter_sweep_2026-10-28/).

Why: the output filter (HS-G15g) trusts every registered position alike (sigma 0.02 m in every direction), but a
scan's registration is constrained unevenly: in a corridor or on open ground one horizontal direction rests on few
surfaces. The registration's final Gauss-Newton Hessian H (6x6, averaged over its residuals, [t_world; r_world]) says
how well: C = translation block of H^-1 is the marginal translation covariance up to a scale. rko_lio 0404f95 logs H
with each filter update (output_filter_log; no output changes), and tools/output_filter_replay_adaptive.cpp replays the
filter (the prediction is OutputPositionFilter's; mode "fixed" reproduces it exactly: difference 0 on spx-3) with
  scalar    R = sigma^2 clip((s / s_ref)^alpha) I
  shape     R = sigma^2 V clip((lambda / mean(lambda))^alpha) V^T
  marginal  R = sigma^2 V clip((lambda / s_ref)^alpha) V^T
(C = V diag(lambda) V^T, s = trace(C) / 3, s_ref the geometric mean of s over this and all earlier registered scans:
causal; clip limits each factor to [lo, hi]; a scan without a Hessian keeps sigma^2 I).

Runs: HS-G15g + output_filter_log (runs/g15glog.yaml, ws/rko_g31, offline_node md5 63a485d0..., label hsp_g15glog) on
the 15 HS development missions. Each must give HS-G15g's trajectory: the real G15g runs byte for byte where they exist
(spx-3, arc-6), the G15g replays of filter_sweep_2026-10-28/ to 1 um elsewhere.
Grid (q_a 0.003 and q_b 1e-5 as HS-G15g): mode scalar / shape / marginal x alpha 0.5 / 1 x [lo, hi] [0.25, 4] /
[0.1, 10] x sigma 0.01 / 0.02 / 0.03 (36 settings), and HS-G15g's own setting first.
Selection, on the tuning half of filter_sweep_2026-10-28/ only (heap-1, pil-2, con-1, eig-2, arc-3, snow-3, arc-5,
arc-4), before any held-out mission is replayed: the setting with the lowest mean ATE (without reference spikes);
among those within 0.1 mm of it the simplest (sigma 0.02 first, then alpha 0.5, then [0.25, 4], then scalar before
shape before marginal). It is a candidate only if its tuning mean is at least 0.1 mm below HS-G15g's; then it is
written into rko_lio and judged under a rule fixed before the held-out half (spx-3, spx-1, arc-1, con-2, con-3, ice-1,
arc-6) is replayed.
