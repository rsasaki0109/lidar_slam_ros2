Screen of registration options on the Hesai XT-32 + STIM320 rig (HS), GrandTour development missions (2026-10-27).
Written before any of these runs.

Why: HS is better than Livox on most development missions (chapter 31.6), but
  - its scan-to-scan vertical jitter is 2-3 times BIEVR-LIO's on the same missions (RMS of the position minus its
    1-s moving average, z: ARC-5 18.5 vs 6.0 mm, CON-3 15.3 vs 5.9, PIL-2 18.0 vs 9.5; no reference used), and
  - it has transient failures: ARC-5 52-57 s (z off by up to 0.19 m, alternating scan to scan), CON-3 444-449 s
    (drifts 0.17 m while the robot stands), PIL-2 and ARC-1 at the start.
  The same 3x jitter against BIEVR-LIO was seen on GEODE (docs/research/geode-hard-evaluation-2026-10.md), where
  Levenberg-Marquardt steps reduced it.

What: G12 on HS (base, dev_G12_hesai_stim, already run) and these variants, each a set of rko_lio keys on top of
G12 (rko_lio 400fc3c build, ws/rko_g13; at G12's keys it gives the same trajectories as ws/rko_g12):
  base      G12 with scan_statistics (per-scan diagnostics; the trajectory stays G12's)
  lm        icp_levenberg_marquardt: true
  nograv    min_beta: 1e9 (no gravity regularisation of the registration's roll and pitch)
  lmnograv  lm + nograv
  p2pl      icp_tangential_weight: 0 (point-to-plane)
  lmp2pl    lm + nograv + p2pl
  bump      bump_image_registration: true with the ENWIDE v10 fallback (2 deg, 3 of 10)
  iw        inertial_window: true (10 s)
Missions (5 of the 16 development missions): ARC-5, CON-3, PIL-2 (the failures above), HEAP-1, SPX-3 (no failure).

Reported per run: ATE without reference spikes (p1_grandtour_ate.py) and the vertical / horizontal jitter
(no reference).

This is a screen, not an adoption: it only picks which variant goes forward. A candidate is adopted only under a
rule fixed before its runs, on all 16 development missions (HS) and on the Phase 1 development data (G regression).
