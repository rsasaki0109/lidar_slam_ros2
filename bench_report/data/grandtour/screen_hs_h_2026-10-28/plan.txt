Eighth screen on the HS rig: hold the start while the robot stands (2026-10-28, written before any of these runs).

Why: HS-G15c's SNOW-3 error sits in its first seconds. Second by second, the prism moves 6-8 cm in the first 8 s (the
robot shifts on the spot), but the estimate wanders 15-20 cm and comes back once the robot walks (FAST-LIO2 stays
within 2 cm). Without the first 9 s the ATE is 15.0 mm instead of 26.8 (FAST-LIO2 16.1, BIEVR-LIO 15.7, which has the
same fault: 36.9 with them). Around a robot standing on a flat snow field the people walking past are what the
registration follows (31.7). The test mission SNOW-2 is at the same site and also starts on the spot (moving start).
rko_lio already has the start-standstill handling (31.7, rko_lio 400fc3c, default off): within start_standstill_radius
of the start, an interval whose IMU shows no rotation and no shaking re-estimates the biases and holds the velocity at
zero; with start_standstill_hold the pose is kept too (the scan is not registered, nor added to the map). On G12 (31.7)
the hold took SNOW-3 0.1161 -> 0.0442 m but broke ARC-3 at its stairs (3.96 m); HS-G15c is far more robust on ARC-3
(0.0190), so it is tried again on it.
Variants (ws/rko_g21; HS-G15c + start_standstill_radius 1.0 + start_standstill_hold true; values as in 31.7, not tuned):
  ss  = the above;
  ssm = the above + map_update_min_translation 0.1 (no map update before the sensor has moved 0.1 m since the last).
Missions: snow-3 (the target), arc-5, arc-4, ice-1, arc-6 (moving starts), arc-3 (the earlier break), arc-1, pil-2,
heap-1 (static starts with start transients). Compared with HS-G15c. Reported: ATE without reference spikes, jitter,
the standstill interval count from the log. A screen: a candidate it picks is judged on all 15 HS missions under a rule
fixed before those runs.

The hold does what it should on SNOW-3 (the first 8 s now within 4 cm instead of 20 cm) and the standstill bias helps
the static starts (heap-1 -2.8, pil-2 -1.2 mm), but ss loses on ICE-1 (+3.5 mm) and ARC-6 (+2.7 mm), beyond the
10 % / 2 mm tolerance; ssm also loses ARC-3 (+5.4 mm). The standstill intervals were real stands (IMU: acceleration
magnitude std 0.05-0.08 m/s^2 and gyro below 0.02 rad/s there, 1-5 m/s^2 once walking). After a moving start the
frame is tilted (ice-1 1.5 deg, arc-6 0.2, snow-3 2.8, arc-4 2.9, arc-5 16) and the standstill bias update takes that
tilt for an accelerometer bias in the body frame, which is wrong once the robot turns: rko_lio d0e9027 instead levels
the frame while it stands (ninth screen).
