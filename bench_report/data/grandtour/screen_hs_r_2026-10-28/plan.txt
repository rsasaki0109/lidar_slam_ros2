written before these runs).

Why: submission 5 (HS-G15g) scored 0.0426 m on the test, with arc-2 at 0.102. HS-G15g's own arc-2 output (no reference
read) jumps up 0.63 m in 0.6 s at t = 143 s (vertical speed 1.86 m/s, impossible for the walking robot), where the
Livox submissions stay level: the registration slipped in height in the ARCHE building. No development mission shows
such a jump with HS-G15g (the largest |vz| on the dev ARC missions is 0.98 m/s for a 0.23 m step on arc-3's stairs).
The idea is a GT-free guard that bounds how far the registration may move a scan from its IMU prediction (in height
first) and keeps such scans out of the map. Its threshold must come from development data only, with margin.
rko_lio 3403690 logs each scan's predicted (initial-guess) position with its registered one (output_filter_log 'U'
events; no output changes).

Amendment (2026-10-28, after the first guard run, before any development mission was judged): the diagnostic run on
the test's arc-2 (ws/rko_g34, rko_lio 82da285, label hsrg_g15gilog) reported 54 bounded scans, yet its logged
innovations reach the limit only once (192.5 s, -250 mm), and its trajectory leaves submission 5's already at 0.3 s.
The cause is in the helper: clamp_innovation returned predicted + (registered - predicted) for every scan, which
rounds off the registered value where the two differ by more than a factor of two (coordinates near the origin);
such a scan was counted as bounded and kept out of the map. 53 of the 54 were such rounding cases. That run therefore
does not show the guard: it went apart from 0.3 s on through those map gaps, and the slip at 142.9 s did not happen in
it at all (largest innovation near it +211 mm at 144.8 s), so it says nothing about whether the guard catches the
slip. It is void, as is the partial development run it was followed by (arc-3, stopped before its registration).

Result (2026-10-28; ws/rko_g35, rko_lio d40d3b9; the test diagnostic, GT-free: only our own outputs and logs):
  arc-2 (hsrh_g15gilog): the trajectory is submission 5's byte for byte up to 142.9 s. The limits bound 2 scans:
  142.9 s (+510 -> +250 mm) and 143.1 s (-250 mm: the prediction, carried by the bounded step's velocity, overshoots).
  The next scans register at the same height as before (IMU position 1.73-1.76 m at 143.5-144.5 s against 1.72-1.74 m
  in submission 5); from 145 s on the two runs differ by 0-10 mm in height (37 mm at 250 s). The guard bounds the step
  but the height does not then follow the IMU: the diagnostic does not show what the guard was made for.
  Reading of the event (GT-free; our own outputs of arc-2 and arc-7, the development reference of arc-3): the step is
  most likely the registration coming back from a height drift built up on the stairs, not a slip.
  - After the step the upper floor lies 2.54 m above the floor the climb started from (-0.41 -> 2.13 m, prism). The
    floor spacing in the ARCHE building is 2.53 m on the development reference (arc-3 dwells at 0.13 and 2.66 m) and
    in our own test arc-7 (climb 0.37 -> 2.90 m). Without the step it would be about 2.0 m.
  - The later descent (2.10 -> -0.35 m at 320-360 s, no step) ends at the level the climb started from (-0.39 against
    -0.41 m).
  - In the 12 s before the step the height sank from 1.87 to 1.60 m at places the later passes (180-200 s) show as
    floor at 2.13 m.
  The IMU check of step 1 (142.0-144.5 s) showed only that the robot did not move vertically during the step, which
  holds either way. Integrating the IMU over longer windows drifts by 0.1 m/s within seconds (attitude errors leak
  gravity) and cannot tell where the drift built up.
  The development runs were stopped after the diagnostic (only arc-3 had begun; removed): G15gi is withdrawn, not
  judged. Its premise, a slip kept by the map, does not hold for the one event it was made for, and bounding a
  correction would only delay it. rko_lio keeps the option (registration_innovation_limit_xy / _z, default 0: off).
  What arc-2 needs instead is to keep the height during the climb (about 100-130 s). There the registration's height
  is weakly constrained: the marginal z std from the logged Hessians is 11-14 (arbitrary units) against 1.0-1.5 on
  the floor after. Development arc-3 and arc-1 reach the same values on their stairs without drifting. Not addressed
  here.
