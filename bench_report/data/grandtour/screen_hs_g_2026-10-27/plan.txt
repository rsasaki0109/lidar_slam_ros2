before any of these runs, while the sixth screen still runs).

Why: in the sixth screen the filter fails on ARC-5, a moving start whose world frame came out 16 deg from level:
mgof 0.0524 m (G15b 0.0152), all of it in the first 20 s (xy 134.5 mm there, then as G15b); of 0.0269 m (max 0.228 m).

acceleration carries a 2.7 m/s^2 bias that its acceleration-bias state (0.05 m/s^2 prior, 1e-4 m^2/s^5 random walk)
cannot learn, and the acceleration jumps by as much when the gravity changes. rko_lio 9461e71 keeps the output
unfiltered after a moving start until the gravity is estimated again, and starts the filter at that pose; without
the window it stays off for the whole run. Static starts are unchanged.
