Fourteenth screen on the HS rig: the start hold within 5 cm with the gyro bias only, or no bias update (2026-10-28,
written before any of these runs; the G15d confirmation is still running).

Why: in the G15d confirmation (decision_rule_G15d.txt) SPX-3, a static start, gives 0.0390 m against G15c's 0.0077.
The output differs from G15c's by 2-9 cm in episodes of 5-25 s and returns to 2-3 mm between them (the registration
chain is nearly the same); the episodes come at turns, and in the body frame the offset points mostly forward. The
standing intervals of a static start replace the accelerometer bias with their mean specific force minus gravity in
the registered attitude: an attitude error of 0.1 deg becomes a horizontal bias of 0.017 m/s^2 in the body frame. The
output filter's bias is a world-frame random walk; after a turn the body-frame error points elsewhere and the filter
needs seconds to follow. rko_lio d15e900 adds start_standstill_accel_bias (default true): when false, the standing
intervals set the gyro bias only and the accelerometer bias stays as the initialization left it (along gravity). Tests
115/115.
Variants (ws/rko_g27, offline_node md5 8464c60c...; not tuned):
  r5ig = G15d + start_standstill_accel_bias false;
  r5n  = G15d with start_standstill_bias none (the hold within 5 cm and zero velocity only; the eleventh screen's p0
         had no bias update with the full hold: heap-1 0.0236, pil-2 0.0259, arc-1 0.0123, arc-3 0.0224).
Missions: spx-3 (the failure), heap-1, pil-2, arc-1, arc-3 (static starts, where the bias update helped r5i), and
ice-1 (a moving start: after a moving start G15d updates no bias, so both variants must give G15d's ice-1 byte for
byte, hsm_r5is/ice-1, md5 20690a28...). Compared with G15c and G15d. Reported: ATE without reference
spikes, jitter. A screen: a candidate it picks is judged on all 15 HS missions under a rule fixed before the runs on
the remaining missions (con-1, eig-2, spx-1, con-2, con-3, and snow-1 reported only).
