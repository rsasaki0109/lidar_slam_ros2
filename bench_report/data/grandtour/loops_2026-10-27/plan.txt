Loop closure with a causal output on GrandTour (2026-10-27, written before any of these runs). Exploratory: it shows
whether loops help the COMFORT configuration, it adopts nothing.

Frontend: HS-G15 (configs/sota_v7/rko_lio_grandtour_hs_G15_2026-10-27.yaml, ws/rko_g15), the submission-3 frontend.
Backend: graph_slam_offline_runner (ws/slam_dev build of lidar_slam_ros2 8f171e9 or later) with
causal_trajectory_output, through p3_grandtour_backend.sh, which now passes the LiDAR extrinsic to the runner (the first
SPX-3 test placed every Hesai scan as if it were in the IMU frame, turned by 90 deg; all its loop candidates came out
2-8 m off and were rejected). Presets: walk60 (lidarslam_mid360_rko_graph_walking.yaml as it is: loops only after 60 m
of travel) and walk20 (the same with distance_loop_closure 20).
Missions (development, revisits found without the reference): con-3, heap-1, arc-1, spx-1, pil-2, con-2.
Reported per run: frontend ATE, causal ATE, the non-causal optimized ATE (for comparison only), loop edges, the worker's
largest lag behind a submap's stamp.

(8,088 full Hesai scans next to the mission's bag), the recorder stalled and the frontend dropped frames; no run of
