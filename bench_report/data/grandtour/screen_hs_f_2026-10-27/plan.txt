Sixth screen on the HS rig: a causal output filter (2026-10-27, written before any of these runs).

Why: splitting the ATE of HS-G15 into bands (residual at 10 Hz: below 1 s, 1-10 s, and the 10-s moving average) shows
its sub-second part about twice BIEVR-LIO's on every development mission tried (mm: arc-4 8.0 / 3.9, eig-2 7.2 / 3.9,
ice-1 4.6 / 2.4, arc-1 11.7 / 7.1, arc-5 7.0 / 2.7, pil-2 13.3 / 5.6, spx-3 5.5 / 2.7, heap-1 6.6 / 4.3, con-3 6.0 /
2.9), while the slower parts are alike: each scan's registered position carries a few millimetres of noise that an
IMU-fused estimator smooths. A prototype offline (HS-G15's trajectories, the missions' STIM320 data, rko_lio's gravity:
(0, 0, g) after a static start; the mission's mean specific force stood in for a moving start's) with a 9-state
filter (world position, velocity, acceleration bias; IMU-driven, the registered positions as measurements) halved the
sub-second band everywhere; its ATE (raw -> filtered, mm): arc-1 16.8 -> 13.4, pil-2 31.7 -> 29.7, eig-2 30.2 -> 28.9,
heap-1 24.2 -> 23.9, arc-5 18.0 -> 17.4, arc-4 20.0 -> 20.0, ice-1 11.5 -> 11.7, arc-6 9.7 -> 9.6, snow-3 88.9 ->
87.8. Its values (position sigma 0.02 m, acceleration noise 0.001 m^2/s^3, bias noise 1e-4 m^2/s^5) were picked on
those prototype runs, from a handful of settings.
rko_lio 52f5609 adds the filter (output_position_filter.hpp, output_filter_position_sigma, 0: off): it runs in
the IMU callback and after each registration, and filters only the output (published odometry and the dumped
trajectory); the registration chain and the map are unchanged. It is causal: each output pose uses the data up to its
own time.
Variants (ws/rko_g20): of = G15b + the filter; mgof = G15b + moving_start_gravity_window_sec 5.0 + the filter (on a
moving start the filter's acceleration depends on the gravity direction).
Missions: arc-1, pil-2, eig-2, heap-1 (static starts), arc-5, arc-4, ice-1, arc-6 (moving starts). Compared with G15b
(hsd_sd3 / G15b_g15b) and, for mgof, with the fifth screen's mg5. Reported: ATE without reference spikes, jitter, and
the band split. A screen: a candidate it picks is judged on all 15 HS missions under a rule fixed before those runs.
Check: ws/rko_g20 with the filter off (G15b's file, check/g15b.yaml) on spx-3 must give G15b's trajectory byte for byte.
