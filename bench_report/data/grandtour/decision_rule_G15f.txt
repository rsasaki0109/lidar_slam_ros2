the run is G15c's. After a moving start: while the robot stands within 1 m of its start, a scan's registered position
is clamped to 5 cm around where it first stood (and not added to the map), the biases stay at zero, the gravity is
estimated again from 5 s of motion for the output filter only, and the output filter runs from then on. It is G15e
(decision_rule_G15e.txt, not adopted) without the parts that act on static starts: on static starts those were a wash
and cost con-2 2.1 mm on four-run means, while the moving-start part carried the clear gain. A configuration for the
HS rig of GrandTour only, like G15c. Two of the six test missions (SNOW-2, SPX-2) start moving.
