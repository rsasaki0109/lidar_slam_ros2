Change (graph_based_slam, this commit; map_save_points_per_pass, default 2,000,000; 0 = the old save):
  map_saver::voxelGridFilterStreamed: when the map is only saved (not published) and the dynamic object filter is off,
  it is not assembled. The submap clouds, each moved by its pose as before, are read once for the bounding box and the
  points per z layer of voxels, then once per pass; a pass takes a range of z layers with at most 2,000,000 points.
  The voxels and their order are those of one VoxelGrid pass over the assembled map (the same float arithmetic places a
  point; the 64-bit voxel index grows slowest with the z layer). A voxel's point is the mean of its points, summed in
  double in the submaps' order; VoxelGrid sums in float in the order of its sort, so the means differ in the last bits
  and map.pcd is no longer byte-identical. Unit tests: against VoxelGrid over the assembled map (same voxels and order,
  within 1e-4 m), against voxelGridFilterInParts for a map too large for VoxelGrid's 32-bit index, and the same bytes
  whatever the passes.

Reproducibility plan (step R, fixed 2026-10-30 before any run with it): why the pipeline's outputs differ from run to
run, and a default that gives the same outputs.
Found first, from the runs above (no new run): every difference is at the start. Across the eight Oxford runs and the
three exp04 runs, the submap stamps differ only in the first submap (0.1-0.3 s later: the backend missed the first one
or two scans) and, in three Oxford runs, in the next five (one scan earlier each); from submap 6 on they are the same.
The frontend publishes before graph_based_slam has subscribed. The launch file can wait for it
(wait_for_output_subscribers, chapter 18.3; the measurements since chapter 18 used it), but not by default, and the
chapter 16 tools used here (pipe_pipeline.sh without BARRIER=1) did not. The offline node can also wait by itself until
its odometry and scan publishers have subscribers (output_subscriber_timeout_sec, polled every 10 ms); the launch sets it
only together with the external barrier script (about 10 s, chapter 18.3).
Runs (graph_based_slam 958b525 in ws/slam_dev, rko_lio ws/rko_g35, the backend settings of step 1b):
  A: the frontend's own wait (output_subscriber_timeout_sec 30, no barrier script): exp04 three times, Oxford three
     times.
  If A's reading holds, the launch file makes the offline node wait by default (graph_based_slam is always launched),
