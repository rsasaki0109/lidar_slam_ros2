Plane refinement stability: screen of two window-BA safeguards (pre-registered)
2026-11-17, after the diagnosis below and before any code of the variants exists. Scope chosen by
the maintainer ("recommended": screen both, development ground truth only).

Diagnosis (quantized-store study, bench_report/data/sz3_cloud_store_2026-11-17)
graph_based_slam's map refinement (hba_pyramid.hpp, plane_ba.hpp) solves windows of 16 submaps
(stride 8) in turn. A window's cost is the plane cost plus an absolute prior of every pose to its
pose-graph pose (0.30 m, 0.035 rad); there is no factor between submaps, and a window's first pose
is fixed at the previous window's result. On GrandTour CON-1 (lidarslam.yaml) the refinement moves
adjacent submaps 47 and 48 by +0.46 m and -0.54 m in z (the odometry's z is flat), a 1 um change of
the stored scans moves its output by up to 0.77 m, and against the development reference the
refined trajectory is worse than the pose graph's (ATE 0.450 against 0.384 m; 0.746 against
0.546 m in 110-160 s). The window is still accepted: acceptance only asks for a lower plane cost.

Variants (parameters of the offline runner; default off, so outputs stay byte-identical)
  R0  current refinement.
  RB  adjacency gate: after a window's BA, if any pair of consecutive window poses changed its
      relative transform (body frame, against the pose graph's) by more than 0.10 m or 1.0 deg,
      the window's result is discarded (its poses stay as they were before the window).
      refine_window_max_adjacent_change_m 0.10, refine_window_max_adjacent_change_deg 1.0.
  RA  relative factors: each pair of consecutive window poses gets a factor to its pose-graph
      relative transform (body frame), sigma 0.05 m / 0.0087 rad (0.5 deg), in the same
      Gauss-Newton system as the plane cost and the prior.
      refine_relative_translation_sigma 0.05, refine_relative_rotation_sigma_rad 0.0087.

Runs (recorded backend inputs; development references only; held-out ground truth stays closed)
  C1 GrandTour CON-1 (ms_outdoor/con-1), lidarslam.yaml; prism reference (reference_dev)
  C2 GrandTour CON-2 (ms_outdoor/con-2), lidarslam.yaml; prism reference (reference_dev)
  M1 MapsHD calibration-room trial 1 (dev_loops/rb), walking preset; ground_truth.tum
  H4 HILTI 2022 exp04 (dev_loops/rb), lidarslam.yaml; 7 control points (a weak check)
  S2 RTK-SLAM Stadtgarten 2 (loops_preset), walking preset; surveyed control points
Each with R0, RB, RA, and each again with submap_cloud_max_error_m 1e-6 (a 1 um perturbation).

Metrics
  ATE: trajectory_refined.tum positions interpolated at the reference timestamps inside the run,
       SE(3)-aligned (bench_report/scripts/p1_grandtour_ate.py align_se3), RMS.
  Sensitivity: max position difference of trajectory_refined.tum between a run and its 1 um run.
  Report only: the pose graph's ATE (trajectory_optimized.tum), windows accepted, wall time.

Decision (per variant V in {RB, RA})
  a. C1: ATE(V) <= 1.05 x ATE(pose graph)  (the refinement no longer makes CON-1 worse);
  b. C2, M1, H4, S2: ATE(V) <= 1.02 x ATE(R0) + 0.001 m  (no loss elsewhere);
  c. C1 sensitivity <= 0.10 m.
  V passes if a, b and c hold. If both pass, RB is recommended unless RA's mean ATE(V)/ATE(R0)
  over the five runs is lower by 0.02 or more. If neither passes, nothing is recommended. Either
  way the defaults stay as they are in this step; changing the default refinement is a separate
  decision for the maintainer.
