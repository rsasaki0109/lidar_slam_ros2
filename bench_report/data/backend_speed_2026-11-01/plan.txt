Backend speed, step 1: the map refinement (plane BA). Written 2026-11-01, after the timing below and before any
change to the refinement code.

Why: on KITTI 05 (t4v05, graph_slam_offline_runner after rsasaki0109/lidar_slam_ros2#573) the bag pass takes about
409 s and the refinement after it about 239 s of a 652 s run for 287 s of data. A replay of the refinement alone (its
input dumped once from the runner: 1,132 submaps, 62.6 M points, the poses and the refiner settings) put, over the
first 21 of 141 windows: plane BA 36.3 s, plane association 3.4 s; and 38.4 s for the per-submap voxel downsample of
all submaps.
Seen in the code:
- solvePlaneBa evaluates every plane feature into a full 6P-vector and (6P x 6P) matrix (P = 16 poses: 96 x 96),
  allocated and zeroed per feature and evaluation, then added whole into the system, although a feature fills only
  the blocks of the poses that observe it. The cost-only evaluation (evaluateTotalCost) builds them too and discards
  them.
- downsampleByVoxelCentroid inserts every point into a std::map.

Candidate (one change set, results meant to be bit-identical):
- The cost-only evaluation stops before the derivatives.
- The system is assembled from each feature's observed-pose blocks only (the skipped entries are exact zeros: adding
  +0.0 to an accumulator that started at +0.0 never changes it), with the same per-entry order of additions.
- The voxel downsample sorts (voxel, index) pairs instead of using a std::map, and sums each voxel's points in
  ascending index order as now; the output stays in voxel order.

Checks and reading, fixed now
1. Identity: the replay's digest (every refined pose's bytes and the report lines) must equal the current code's on
   the KITTI 05 dump; the unit tests of the refinement pass; and the eight runs of chapter 42
   (cleanup_runner_check.sh) give byte-identical outputs except the wall-time dependent trajectory_causal.tum.
   Anything else stops the change until explained.
2. Worth: the change is kept if the refinement on the KITTI 05 replay takes at least 20 % less time. The runner's
   KITTI 05 time before and after is reported.
3. Nothing about what the refinement computes changes; parameters and outputs stay as they are.
