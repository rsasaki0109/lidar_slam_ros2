What H8 adds, and its limits
  A third fresh family (selection_h8.txt), fetched from Zenodo. It is a multirotor, a platform no development family
  has. A result on H8 alone is one family and supports no claim; the tier needs two fresh families with the win
  condition holding over them.

Rig (per-rig inputs; rig_pastel.yaml and g_params_pastel.yaml from p8_make_rigs.py)
  LiDAR /os0_128/points_filtered (Ouster OS0-128; the bags hold this topic, not /os0_128/points as the authors' file
  names it), IMU /os1_16/imu (100 Hz, the built-in IMU of the second Ouster): the pair of the authors' FAST-LIO file.
  Tactical-gyro input off. Base frame = that IMU.
  The LiDAR pose in the IMU frame is T_OS1_IMU_OS0 of os0_128_lidar.yaml with one correction: as printed the matrix
  is a reflection (determinant -1, its [2, 2] entry -0.9992). With +0.9992 it is orthonormal and equals the rotation
  between the two sensors in the dataset's own X4_pastel.urdf; and on the pipeline bag the three IMUs read gravity on
  the same axis with the same sign and their gyro signals are related by rotations within 9 degrees of the identity.
  The corrected matrix is used for every method.
  FAST-LIO2: its published ouster64.yaml with scan_line 128 and blind 0.5 as in preregistration.txt (the authors' own
  file uses blind 4; the first run's value is kept for all families).
  Conversion: as in preregistration.txt (points with x = y = z = 0 dropped, the nine Ouster fields packed, stamp
  order). The md5 of the streamed bytes must equal the Zenodo record's (pastel_files.txt). The bags also hold the
  total-station topics /leica/pose and /leica/original/pose: the converter does not deserialise them.

Metric
  ATE RMSE of the prism position (m): rigid SE(3) Umeyama fit without scale, the estimate interpolated at the
  reference timestamps, no time offset estimated. The estimate's prism is T_world_imu * t, with t the prism in the
  OS1-16 IMU frame = T_OS1_IMU_OS0 * inverse(T_Body_OS0) * T_Body_Prism (os0_128_lidar.yaml with the same sign
  correction for T_Body_OS0, leica_prism.yaml). The reference is the total-station trajectory: the per-sequence file of
  ground_truth.zip if it holds one, otherwise the bag's /leica/pose; which one is recorded before any score is
  computed. Scores per sequence = median of three repetitions; H8's geometric means and G's worst ratio to the best
  rival as in preregistration.txt.
