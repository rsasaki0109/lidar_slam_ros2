What H9 adds, and its limits
  A fourth fresh family and the first with a car. With H8 PASTEL (scored; its win condition is not met as written,
  on the completion clause of sequence 11, preregistration_h8.txt) it makes two fresh families measured. Whatever H9
  gives, the result over the families is reported as it is; H8's result is not changed by anything here.
  Limits: seven drives on four short routes (the ones whose archive is at most 9 GB), no highway, no drive over 5 min.

Rig (rig_odyssey.yaml, g_params_odyssey.yaml from p8_make_rigs.py)
  LiDAR /ouster/points, IMU /m300/imu, base frame = the IMU. The LiDAR pose in the IMU frame is the identity: the
  project site states that all outputs are given in one coordinate system (the LiDAR's) and that no extrinsic
  calibration is needed. Tactical-gyro input on (selection_h9.txt).
  FAST-LIO2: ouster64.yaml with lidar_type 3, timestamp_unit 3, scan_line 128, blind 0.5, as for the other families.
  Conversion (p8_odyssey_stream_to_ros2.py; the same bag for every method): the tar.gz is streamed and never stored;
  only ouster/<ns>.bin and m300/imu.txt are read; points without a return (NaN) are dropped; each point is stored as
  x, y, z, t (uint32 ns since the start of the revolution) and ring (the row of the 128 x 2048 grid), 18 bytes.
  Reflectivity and near-infrared are not copied: with them a 221 s drive does not fit /dev/shm (the pipeline sequence
  is 42 MB per second of data at 18 bytes), and no method here uses them for its estimate. The cloud's stamp is the
  file name (taken as the start of the revolution: t counts from it); the IMU's orientation columns are not copied.
  The whole archive must be read (its length equals the share link's).
  refsys/ (the reference) and metadata/ (statistics and a trajectory image) pass through the stream unread; only
  their member names and sizes are listed in the bag summary.

Run protocol
  bench_report/scripts/p8_blind_h9.sh, in the order of h9_sequences.txt, G, R3, R2, R1, R4, three repetitions each.
  A converted bag above 9 GB is not run and is recorded as too large (selection_h9.txt).

Metric
  ATE RMSE of position (m) as in preregistration.txt (rigid SE(3) Umeyama fit without scale, the estimate
  interpolated at the reference stamps, no time offset estimated). The reference is refsys/lidar_poses_ref.txt, the
  pose of the LiDAR at each revolution relative to the first (the devkit's load_ground_truth_poses), stamped with the
  sorted ouster file names. The estimate is every method's T_world_imu; the IMU frame is the LiDAR frame, so no lever
  arm. The reference is fetched only after sealing and the maintainer's approval, by streaming the archives again
  and reading that one member.
  Completion, written for H9 after sequence 11 of H8 (whose reference file spans more time than its bag): a run
  completes if it exits by itself, writes a trajectory without NaN, and spans at least 95 % of the time between the
  first and the last reference stamp that lie within the converted bag's LiDAR stamps. For H9 the reference stamps are
  the LiDAR stamps, so this is 95 % of the drive.
  RTF = wall time / data duration; peak RSS of the method's process. Geometric means, G's ratio to the best rival and
  the win condition as in preregistration.txt, for H9 and over H8 + H9.
