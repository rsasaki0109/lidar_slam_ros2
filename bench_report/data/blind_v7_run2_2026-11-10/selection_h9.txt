Family
  Odyssey (University of Goettingen, arXiv 2512.14428): a car with an Ouster OS1-128 (rev. 7, 10 Hz, 128 x 2048) and
  an iMAR iNAT-M300/TLE-LN1 MEMS IMU at 300 Hz; reference from a ring-laser-gyro INS (iMAR iPRENA-M-II). Twelve routes,
  three drives each, among them tunnels and car parks. One tar.gz per drive on owncloud.gwdg.de
  (odyssey_archives.txt), no account needed. No Odyssey file was ever in this repository's records and no sequence was
  a development sequence. It is a car, the platform H8 and H6's reachable part do not have.

Rule for the sequences, from the archive names and sizes only
  Every drive whose archive is at most 9 GB (the size rule of H6: the converted bag has to sit in /dev/shm beside the
  methods on a VM with 15 GiB), except the pipeline sequence. Pipeline sequence: UndergroundCarPark3 (not blind).
  Blind (h9_sequences.txt): Beltway1, Beltway2, Beltway3, Marketplace3, Tunnel2, UndergroundCarPark1,
  UndergroundCarPark2. Seven sequences on four routes. Left out by size: every drive of CountryRoad, ForestRoad,
  Highway, HighwayTunnel, InnerCity, ParkingGarage, Suburb and Theater, Marketplace1/2, Tunnel1/3. So H9 has no
  highway and no long drive; that is a limit of this VM and is reported with the result.
  As for H6: a converted bag above 9 GB is not run and is recorded as too large.

Tactical-gyro input (the G21 criterion: angular random walk <= 0.2 deg/sqrt(h) and bias instability <= 1 deg/h, from
the manufacturer's figures, decided here)
  The dataset paper's Table 2 gives 0.8 deg/h for this IMU's gyro bias instability. iMAR's tables for the TLE-LN1
  sensor give an angular random walk below 0.08 deg/sqrt(h) and a bias instability "< 1 deg/h" (iATTHEMO TLE-LN1
  sheet) or "< 1.2 deg/h" (a TraceRT MVT-300 table, read through a web summary whose row mapping was garbled). The
  figure the dataset's authors state for the unit they used is taken: 0.08 and 0.8, inside the criterion. On.

What was read to choose
  The paper through web summaries asked for the sensors, the data format, the frames and the table of route lengths
  and durations, and told not to report results: Beltway 221 s / 1.75 km, Marketplace 221 s / 0.87 km, Tunnel 305 s /
  0.47 km, UndergroundCarPark 161 s / 0.30 km (means of the three drives). The project site's front, system and
  download pages (archive sizes); the devkit's README and its two Python files (file layout and loaders). The HTTP
  headers of the 37 share links (name, length). No per-sequence page, no archive byte, no result table of any method.
