Holdouts
  H6 FusionPortable V2. Rule, from the archive names and sizes only (fusionportable_v2_archives.txt): for the handheld,
     legged and ugv platforms, one sequence per scene (the name between the platform and the number): the lowest-
     numbered sequence whose archive is at most 9 GB. This VM has 15 GiB of memory and about 7 GB of free disk; the
     converted bag has to sit in /dev/shm beside the methods. That gives nine sequences (h6_sequences.txt):
       handheld_escalator00, handheld_grass00, handheld_room00,
       legged_grass01 (grass00 is 10.2 GB), legged_room00, legged_transition00, legged_underground00,
       ugv_campus01 (campus00 is 10.0 GB), ugv_parking00.
     Left out by the size rule: handheld_underground (13.8 GB), ugv_transition00/01 (14.8 / 11.5 GB), and every
     vehicle_* archive (11 to 22 GB). ugv_parking02 is the pipeline sequence and is not blind.
     If a converted bag is above 9 GB, the sequence is not run and is recorded as too large; the next-numbered
     sequence of the same platform and scene with an archive of at most 9 GB takes its place if there is one.
  H7 MCD. Rule, from the download page only (mcd_files.txt): for each site and time of day, the sequence with the
     smallest Ouster bag, pipeline sequences and ntu_day_01 excluded (selection.txt). Six sequences:
       ntu_day_02, ntu_night_13, kth_day_10, kth_night_05, tuhh_day_04, tuhh_night_09.
  Conversion (the same input for every method; p8_7z_http_stream.py, p8_ros1_stream_to_ros2.py): the bag is streamed
  and never stored whole; only the LiDAR and the IMU topic are kept; LiDAR points with x = y = z = 0 (no return) are
  dropped and the nine Ouster fields (x, y, z, intensity, t, reflectivity, ring, ambient, range) are stored packed, so
  the cloud is unorganized; messages are written in stamp order. H6: the unpacked size and CRC32 must equal the 7z
  header's. H7: the IMU comes from its own bag; the LiDAR header stamp is moved by -0.1 s, because MCD stamps the end
  of the sweep while `t` counts from its start (the dataset's user manual), and every method here expects the start.
  No groundtruth/, evaluation/ or other-method file of either dataset is downloaded before sealing.

Run protocol
  Machine: this cloud VM (4 vCPU, 15 GiB, Ubuntu 24.04, ROS 2 Jazzy). One method run at a time; G and FAST-LIO2 hold
  an exclusive lock, the offline rivals a shared one (first run). Order: h6_sequences.txt, then H7 as listed. Per
  sequence: G, R3, R2, R1, R4, three repetitions each, then the bag is deleted (bench_report/scripts/p8_blind_h6.sh).
  Each run: exit status, wall time, peak RSS of the method's process, the trajectory (T_world_imu) and its sha256.
  A run that fails for a reason that is not a property of the method (a download, conversion or script fault, a VM
  restart) is rerun after the fix, and the fix is recorded here. A poor or failed trajectory caused by the method stays.

Sealing and the ground truth
  After the last run of a holdout, a manifest with the sha256 of every trajectory, log summary and bag summary is
  committed and pushed (sealed_manifest_h6.txt, sealed_manifest_h7.txt). Only after that is the maintainer asked
  whether to open that holdout's ground truth.

Metrics (scored only after the ground truth is opened)
  ATE RMSE of position (m): rigid SE(3) Umeyama fit without scale, the estimate interpolated at the reference
  timestamps, no time offset estimated. The estimate is every method's T_world_imu, moved to the point the reference
  measures:
    H7: the reference is the pose of the body (IMU) frame (pose_inW.csv, 10 Hz); no lever arm.
    H6: total-station sequences (handheld_*, legged_*) measure the prism; INS sequences (ugv_campus01, ugv_parking00)
        give a 6-DoF pose. The public pages do not say in which frame the files are written. Before any score is
        computed, the frame is taken from the ground-truth files' own headers or the dataset tools if they state it;
        otherwise the IMU origin is compared for the INS sequences and the prism for the total-station sequences, with
        the IMU-to-prism translation of the platform's body_imu.yaml (translation_sensor_leica_marker; the handheld
        file has it commented out, the 20230426 value of the same sensor head is used). The choice is recorded before
        scoring and is the same for all methods; the score under the other choice is reported beside it.
  A method's score on a sequence is the median of its three repetitions.
  Completion: a run completes if it exits by itself, writes a trajectory without NaN, and its trajectory spans at least
  95 % of the reference's time span. A sequence where a method has no complete run is a failure of that method.
  Per holdout and over all sequences run: each method's geometric mean; for G, the number of sequences where it is more
  than 10 % worse than the best rival on that sequence; G's completion, real-time factor (wall time / data duration)
  and peak RSS on every sequence.
  Win condition (the plan's, unchanged): G has the lowest geometric mean among the rivals run, no sequence more than
  10 % worse than its best rival, 100 % completion, RTF at most 0.85 and peak RSS at most 330 MB on every sequence.
  It is evaluated per holdout and over both; only the result over both families counts for the tier.
