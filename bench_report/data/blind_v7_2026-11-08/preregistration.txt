SOTA v7 Phase 5, first blind run: preregistration (fixed 2026-11-08, before any byte of a holdout below is read).

What this run can and cannot show
  docs/roadmap/sota-v7-plan-2026-10.md section 2 says a tier is claimed only with at least two fresh families. Neither
  holdout here is a fresh family: HILTI 2022 (H4) gave the development sequences exp01/exp04/exp07, and GrandTour (H5)
  gave sixteen development missions. So this run is a within-family blind measurement: how the frozen global
  configuration G, chosen on development data, does on unseen sequences of two development families, against rivals
  run on the same inputs. No tier claim and no README claim row follows from it, whatever the scores are. It is
  reported in bench_report/REPORT.md (chapter 57) with every sequence, failed runs included.

Run protocol
  Machine: this cloud VM (4 vCPU, 15 GiB, Ubuntu 24.04, ROS 2 Jazzy). One method run at a time. A run of G or of
  FAST-LIO2 (whose result depends on timing, since the bag is played in real time) takes an exclusive lock, and nothing
  else that uses the CPU runs beside it (other jobs on the VM take the same lock, shared). The offline rivals (R2, R3,
  R4) process every message whatever the load, so other jobs may run beside them; their wall times are then marked as
  measured on a shared VM and are reported only for information.
  Order: H4 exp14, exp16, exp18, then H5 in the order above. Per sequence: G, R3, R2, R1, R4, three repetitions each
  (the SOTA-v6 policy), then the sequence's bag is deleted.
  Each run: exit status, wall time, peak RSS of the method's process (/usr/bin/time -v; for FAST-LIO2 the mapping node,
  not the bag player), the trajectory file and its sha256. A run that fails for a reason that is not a property of the
  method (a conversion or script bug, a VM crash) is rerun after the fix, and the fix is recorded here. A poor or failed
  trajectory caused by the method stays as it is.

Sealing and the ground truth
  After the last run, a manifest with the sha256 of every trajectory, log summary and converted-bag summary is
  committed and pushed (bench_report/data/blind_v7_2026-11-08/sealed_manifest.txt). Only then is the maintainer asked
  once whether to open the ground truth. After a yes: the three HILTI ground-truth files are downloaded and checked
  against the selection file's sha256, and the eleven prism_position references are downloaded.

Metrics (scored only after the ground truth is opened)
  H4: APE RMSE of the position (m) against the dense 6-DoF ground truth in the IMU frame; every method's trajectory is
      the IMU (base) pose. scripts/ape_from_tum.py --interpolate (rigid SE(3) Umeyama fit, no scale; the estimate
      interpolated at the reference timestamps).
  H5: ATE RMSE (m) of T_world_prism against the prism reference, bench_report/scripts/p1_grandtour_ate.py (rigid SE(3),
      no scale, the COMFORT metric); the score with total-station spikes removed is reported beside it.
  A method's score on a sequence is the median of its three repetitions.
  Completion: a run completes if it exits by itself, writes a trajectory without NaN, and its trajectory spans at least
  95 % of the reference's time span. A sequence where a method has no complete run counts as a failure of that method.
  Per holdout (H4, H5) and over all 14 sequences:
    - the geometric mean of the scores of each method;
    - for G: the number of sequences where it is more than 10 % worse than the best rival on that sequence;
    - G's completion; G's real-time factor (node time / data duration) and peak RSS on every sequence.
  The win condition of the plan (unchanged from SOTA-v6) is evaluated and reported as it stands: G has the lowest
  geometric mean among the rivals run, no sequence more than 10 % worse than its best rival, 100 % completion, RTF at
  most 0.85 and peak RSS at most 330 MB on every sequence. Meeting it here would still not be a claim (first section).
